# MelodyArc

> MelodyArc is an enterprise AI operations platform that orchestrates data, business rules, AI agents, and human workers in one governed system, so the right judgment is applied at the right moment. It turns messy, cross-system, high-consequence operational work into modular building blocks with full traceability.

MelodyArc's platform is organized around Points (atomic units of work capturing context, action, state, and ownership), Point Sets (reusable workflow configurations), a Point Engine (a dynamic decision graph routing tasks through rules, automation, AI, and human intervention in real time), AI Operators (configured workers with instructions and tools), a Portal (management/oversight interface), and Record (built-in audit trail). It targets enterprise operations teams and runs on managed or self-hosted infrastructure.

At the time of this profile, MelodyArc publishes no public OpenAPI, machine-readable API reference, or developer registry packages. Developer documentation is a beta single-page app. This llms.txt is generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the public site and repo artifacts.
